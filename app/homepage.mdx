## What is Evolve?

Evolve is a concept for a new way for humans to self-organise and relate. It has
many aspects, and can be framed differently depending on context.

From the simplest, and most familiar perspective, Evolve is a “transformational”
festival; a gathering of people on land to connect with themselves and each
other in a ritual that breaks them out of the routine of everyday life.

Evolve is also a conference. It is a place for people to come together and share
knowledge, wisdom, and skills, especially those which help us to collectively
grow and evolve.

Evolve is a movement. Part of the concept is that Evolve will grow from the
first festival into many festivals around the world, allowing as many people as
possible to experience, even if only for a few days, being out of “the matrix”
and living in a different, richer, more connected way than most of us currently
live.

Evolve is an experiment in decentralised governance and participatory democracy.
We believe in the wisdom of crowds -- the concept that, with the right
frameworks and systems in place, large groups can make better decisions for the
collective than individuals or small groups (in part because the latter are
prone to capture and corruption).

## Why Evolve?

We believe that human civilisation (and to a lesser extent, the biosphere
itself) is coming to a crossroads. We have reached a situation where our
technological power (for example, nuclear weapons, biotech, fossil fuels, AI,
nanotechnology) have surpassed our ability to make good decisions, and as such
we’re faced with the very real possibility of destruction of our civilisation
and perhaps our species itself. This is not to mention the impact we’re already
having on countless other species on the planet.

One solution to this problem is a top-down technocratic dystopia. This might
avoid civilizational collapse, but is likely not a future any of us want for our
children / grandchildren.

On top of merely avoiding catastrophe, we also dream about a future which is
more beautiful, more connected, more kind, and more loving than what we have
today.

We believe that in order to create such a future, we need grass-roots,
decentralised culture change, and better ways to self-govern. As a species, we
need to evolve both individually and collectively.

## Vision

There are multiple layers to the vision for Evolve.

At its most simple, we envisage Evolve becoming a festival that brings people
together to celebrate, share, and learn, hopefully improving the lives of
hundreds or thousands of people. If this is all that Evolve achieves, this would
feel like success.

The next level of the vision is the movement. Here we see the Evolve festival
propagating itself around the globe, and increasing the number of people
impacted by an order of magnitude. These events would work together to support
one another and make collective decisions that improve the Evolve ecosystem for
all involved.

Beyond this, we can imagine Evolve started to seed more permanent intentional
communities of people living together, and modelling their communities on
Evolve’s values and ways of doing governance. These communities would be
part of the greater web, and would also share support and resources with the
events and each other.

Our blue-sky vision for Evolve is that it inspires or becomes a new way for
humans to organise and govern ourselves and steward the planet; one which is
more connective, more attuned, and more alive. We imagine deep culture change
that brings humanity more in harmony with each other and with the planet we live
on and with.

## Values

**__1. Learning, Growing and Evolving Together__**

We believe in the power of adaptive, collaborative cultures that are committed to learning how to transform and evolve collectively.

We support each other’s development as individuals while cultivating an emerging cultural transformation that holds and guides us to become capable of meeting these times *together.*

Personal growth and healing deepen our capacity for collaboration and strengthen the effectiveness of our community action. Our shared purpose provides a powerful motivation to learn, grow, and evolve — both individually and collectively.

We actively share the knowledge, skills, and wisdom we have that help us to make a meaningful, positive difference in the world.

We understand learning to be a lifelong, relational process, and recognise the importance of collaborating across difference. We embrace honest reflection, welcome feedback, make sense of life together and approach experiences with curiosity, humility, and a willingness to be transformed.

**__2. Ecological Wellbeing and Interconnectedness__**

We recognise that we are nature — deeply interdependent with all living systems. This understanding guides us to act with humility, reverence, and responsibility toward the wellbeing of this Earth and all beings.

We listen to ecological wisdom. We attune to the intelligence of ecosystems, honouring local place, cyclical time, and the voices of the more-than-human world. This relational awareness informs our sense-making, design, and action.

We design for sustainability, regeneration and balance, and we implement effective feedback systems. Our choices are guided by a commitment to regeneration over extraction, harmony over dominance, and long-term thriving over short-term gains.

**__3. Belonging and Meaningful Contribution__**

We believe that everyone has inherent worth and a place in the whole, and we practise love, compassion, and non-violence with all beings. 

We honour difference as a source of strength and strive to create spaces where all people feel seen, safe, and valued – accepted as they are, and free to express and explore.

We recognise that we all have different skills, passions and capacities. We support people to find and explore their part to play within Evolve that is personally meaningful and valued by the community.

We co-create cultures of safety, trust, and mutual respect that allow us to grow at our edges, and expand into our wider creative potentials. We offer opportunities for authentic connection and shared experience that strengthen our sense of belonging and deepen our collective capacity.

**__4. Celebration, Creativity and Play__**

As we turn to face the challenges of our time, it is more vital than ever to celebrate life and cultivate joy, gratitude, and an appreciation of beauty. These practices sustain us, deepen our resilience, and help us embody the wonder and appreciation of this precious life. 

Creativity sparks innovation, strengthens our lifeforce, nourishes resilience, and brings new possibilities into being. We invite exploration and experimentation to discover the connections and potentials that will help reveal the path forward.

Play builds trust and collaboration, accelerates learning, stimulates creativity and opens space for emergence. We harness the power of play to develop our capacity for openness, curiosity, and joyful creativity.

**__5. Synthesis and Collective Sensemaking__**

We commit to seeking truth together — not as a fixed destination, but as an ongoing, shared process of dialogue, exploring diverse views, and integrating perspectives. This collective sensemaking helps us better understand what is unfolding in the world, including the root causes of disharmony, and how to respond with creativity, integrity, and care.

We strive to understand the individual and cultural challenges to good sensemaking. We create spaces and systems that support generative dialogue, mutual understanding and conflict resolution. We practise humility and self-reflection, aiming to listen deeply, reflect honestly, and meet conflict in ways that build trust rather than erode it.

We honour the dance of opposites, seeking integration over competition. We synthesise diverse perspectives into deeper, shared understanding — recognising that it is through integration, not necessarily agreement, that collective intelligence emerges. We hold complexity with openness and curiosity, allowing something wiser to emerge through the tension of paradox.

We take time to reflect before acting, considering the broader context and long-term consequences. This practice supports us to act with greater clarity and coherence, and align our insight with action in service of transformation.

**__6. Collective Governance and Decision Making__**

We seek to understand and implement governance and decision-making systems that best serve Evolve’s mission and are guided by its core values. Our structures are designed to be responsive, adaptive, and in service to the collective good.

We aim for a fair and equitable distribution of power and agency within the Evolve organism — minimising centralisation wherever possible. We recognise that inclusion leads to greater trust, wisdom, and accountability.

We prioritise transparency, active feedback loops, and clear checks on power. We recognise that good governance depends on meaningful participation, honest reflection, and systems that support continuous learning and evolution.

**__7. Agency and Action__**

We understand that creating positive change in the world can be quiet, slow, and subtle, as well as bold, clear, and tangible. We seek to be part of transformation across this full spectrum, recognising that all forms of meaningful action matter.

We use our shared sensemaking and inspiration to generate ideas for real-world projects we believe can make a difference. Drawing on our collective skills, talents, and resources, we bring these ideas into action. 

We take time to synthesise diverse perspectives, and consider the broader context and potential impacts of our actions, allowing shared understanding to foster wise, grounded action.

We reflect on and learn from our projects and experiments in an ongoing, iterative process. By integrating insights and experiences we continually refine how we participate in positive change, together.
