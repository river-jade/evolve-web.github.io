The village we ran at Spring ConFest in 2024 was somewhat of an experiment. It was
a very small ConFest (around 1200 people), and our first village. It was such
a huge success, that we decided to do it again for Easter, this time with a much
bigger kitchen and lounge space. 

We hosted a brunch for everyone, a Shabbat dinner on the Friday night, and a 
beautiful Kirtan on Sunday evening, as well as a full timetable of workshops.

Incredibly happy with how this all went, and excited to see you all at next
ConFest in Spring!!

Sadly we were having too much fun to take any photos this time, so please let us
know if you were there and have some that we can publish!
