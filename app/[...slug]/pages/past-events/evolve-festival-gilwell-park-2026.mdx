We held our second festival at Gilwell Park in March 2026, welcoming around 200 
people for a rich weekend of connection, learning, celebration, and co-creation.

The gathering felt deeply alive. Across the weekend, there was a strong program 
of thoughtful, high-quality workshops, beautiful music and evening entertainment, 
and many opportunities to explore the values, relationships, and ways of being 
that sit at the heart of Evolve.

It was heartening to see the festival become not just an event, but a living 
expression of the culture we want to help grow: more connected, more conscious, 
more collaborative, and more rooted in care for each other and the wider world.

We’re excited to keep bringing people together on the land in service of cultural 
transformation and a more beautiful future.
