The Evolve Vision Conference is a small (20-30) gathering of people who have expressed serious interest in the Evolve project,
or who have deep experience with building community organisations. It will be a two day gathering on the land at Moora Moora,
and an opportunity to share the larger vision for Evolve more broadly, as well as integrate different perspectives for a
confluence of vision. It's also the next step in expanding the democratic decision making that is a deep intention of the
Evolve project.
