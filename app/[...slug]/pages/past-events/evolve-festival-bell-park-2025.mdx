We ran our inaugural festival at Bell Park in March, 2025 for around 150 
attendees. 

We couldn't have been happier with how it went. We had a full 
timetable of high-quality workshops, fantastic night time entertainment,
and had to chance to dive into some of the underlying values and vision of 
the Evolve project. 

We're excited to continue bringing people together on land 
for the purpose of transforming culture and building a better world.
