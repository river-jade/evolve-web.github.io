We held our first village at the inaugural Spring ConFest in 2024. This was our
first "public" event, and it felt appropriate to run it at ConFest, which is a 
primary inspiration for the Evolve vision.

The village was very well attended and we got lots of very positive feedback.

There were tons of high quality workshops in the space, and the timetable was full 
for the whole festival.
