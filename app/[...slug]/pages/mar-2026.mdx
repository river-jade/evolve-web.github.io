## What is Evolve?

Evolve is more than a festival — it’s a movement. It’s a gathering of hearts and
minds, united by a shared vision: to create a better world for human and
non-human beings alike. Join us for a lived experience of deeper connection—to
yourself, to others, and to the land. Together, we’ll plant the seeds for a
global network of interconnected communities and celebrate the beauty of life,
humanity, and nature.

[Read more about Evolve on our main page](/).

## Our Vision

- ✨ Share knowledge and wisdom for positive change.
- ✨ Explore how to live in greater harmony with the earth and each other.
- ✨ Celebrate the joy of community, creativity, and collaboration.

## What to Expect

- Radical co-creation and self-reliance—come as a participant, leave as a
  contributor.
- Facilities include drinking water, toilets, and showers.
- Stunning natural surroundings with opportunities for forest walks
  and grounding in nature.
- Talking circles for storytelling, connection, and reflection.
- Workshop spaces, community kitchens, and a cosy lounge area.
- A like-minded community dedicated to creating positive change.

## Accommodation

Camping only — bring your tent and enjoy sleeping under the stars. Van and car
camping also welcome! Please let us know if you want to bring a caravan as space
may be limited.

## Cost

- 🐦 Early Bird (until 31st Jan): Adults $90, Kids $55.
- 🎟️ Standard Price: Adults $140, Kids $85.
- 👶 Under 5s: Free.

Prices are for 3 nights. Part of your contribution is also a minimum of 2 hours of your time to help 
things to run smoothly. Please contact us if you have financial hardship but would really like to come. 
NTAFLOF (no-one turned away for lack of funds).

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Expandable title="Where is the festival located?">
The festival is held at Gilwell Park, Gembrook. It is approximately 1 hour from Melbourne.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What kind of accommodation is available? Can I camp in my van / bus / caravan?">
This is a camping-only event. Please bring your own tent and bedding. Van and car camping are welcome, but please notify us if you plan to bring a caravan or bus as space is limited. \ \n\nPlease note: vehicles can not be moved in the camping area during the event without the supervision of a traffic marshall. Please notify us if you're camping with your vehicle and \ need to leave early or arrive late.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Are there facilities on site?">
Yes. Facilities include drinking water, toilets, hot showers, community kitchen (BYO camp stove / esky), and a cosy lounge area.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Is this a family-friendly event?">
Absolutely. Children are welcome. Tickets for kids are $55 (early bird) or $85 (standard). Children under five attend for free.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Do I need to volunteer?">
Yes. Evolve is a co-created community event. Part of your contribution is a minimum of 2 hours of your time to help the festival run smoothly.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What should I bring?">
Bring your camping gear, clothes for all weather, a water bottle, and an open heart. Since it's a self-reliant gathering, ensure you have everything you need for a comfortable stay.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I bring my pet?">
Sorry, with the exception of service animals, Gilwell Park doesn't allow pets onsite.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Do facilitators / performers / crew etc get free tickets?">
Part of Evolve's philosophy is that it is a co-creation. Currently, everyone contributes financially to the infrastructure costs of the festival, and everyone \ contributes in ways which feel meaningful and aligned for them.  Currently, no one gets paid (apart from 3rd party contractors) and no one gets comp tickets.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Are there any day passes?">
In order to hold a strong container (as well as to minimise admin overhead), Evolve does not currently offer day passes.
</Expandable>
