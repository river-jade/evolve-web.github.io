## What is Evolve?

Evolve is more than a festival—it’s a movement. It’s a gathering of hearts and
minds, united by a shared vision: to create a better world for human and
non-human beings alike. Join us for a lived experience of deeper connection—to
yourself, to others, and to the land. Together, we’ll plant the seeds for a
global network of interconnected communities and celebrate the beauty of life,
humanity, and nature.

[Read more about Evolve](/).

## Our Vision

- ✨ Share knowledge and wisdom for positive change.
- ✨ Explore how to live in greater harmony with the earth and each other.
- ✨ Celebrate the joy of community, creativity, and collaboration.

## What to Expect

- Radical co-creation and self-reliance—come as a participant, leave as a
  contributor.
- Facilities include drinking water, toilets, and showers.
- Stunning natural surroundings with opportunities for swimming, forest walks,
  and grounding in nature.
- A fire circle for storytelling, connection, and reflection.
- Workshop spaces, community kitchens, and a cosy lounge area.
- A like-minded community dedicated to creating positive change.

## Workshops & Experiences

Evolve is an “unconference”, which means that anyone can offer a workshop in an
area aligned with the vision of the festival. We’ll also be inviting some
well-respected facilitators in these disciplines and will announce more as we
can.

## Entertainment

🎧 Dance the night away with DJs on Saturday night (music ends at midnight). 🎤
On Sunday, enjoy an open mic night where the community takes the stage.

## Accommodation

Camping only—bring your tent and enjoy sleeping under the stars. Van and car
camping also welcome! Please let us know if you want to bring a caravan as space
may be limited.

## Cost

Adult tickets are $130 for 3 nights, and kids $80 (under fives free). Part of
your contribution is also a minimum of 2 hours of your time to help things to
run smoothly. Please contact us if you have financial hardship but would really
like to come. NTAFLOF (no-one turned away for lack of funds).

## Not-for-Profit

Evolve is a community-driven, not-for-profit event. Your participation directly
